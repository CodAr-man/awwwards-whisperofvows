The Atelier Heritage

A Legacy of
Uncompromising Elegance

Established in 2012, Whisper of Vows Boutique was envisioned to bring the pinnacle of European haute couture to discerning brides. We transform the search for your gown into an intimate, bespoke journey of discovery.

Housing a meticulously curated archive of over 400 designer masterpieces, each creation in our salon represents the height of sartorial craftsmanship—fashioned from peerless lace and opulent silks to ensure your bridal moment is nothing short of legendary.

Book Your Appointment

We're So Excited to
Welcome You

During your 1.5-hour wedding dress appointment (or 45 minutes for shoes / accessories), we'll give you our full attention and top-notch service to make sure you feel special every step of the way.

Your paid consultation includes:

  • Private access to the boutique — no overlapping appointments.
  • Dedicated bridal expert
  • Over 400 gowns from top European designers to explore.
  • Try on up to 5-6 gowns (send us your choices ahead of time — click here to select).
  • With your consent, we'll capture and AirDrop photos and videos of your favourites during your appointment.
  • We'll guide you through fit, alterations, accessories, and more.
  • You are welcome to bring up to 6 guests.
  • The fee is credited if you "Say Yes!" at your appointment.
